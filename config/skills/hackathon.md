Domain: hackathon management. A team has a name, college, city, track (AI, Web, Health, Sustainability, Open), a project title and a stage (Registered, Shortlisted, Finalist, Eliminated). A team has members (name, email, phone, role Leader / Developer / Designer / Presenter), mentor sessions (mentor, topic, date, time, status Scheduled / Completed / Cancelled) and judge scores (judge, innovation, technical depth, presentation and impact, each 1 to 10, and comments). Team names are unique, member emails are unique, phone numbers are 10-digit Indian mobiles and every score is between 1 and 10 (400 otherwise). Dashboard: teams by track and stage, members per team, mentor sessions done, average innovation score.
