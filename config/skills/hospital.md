Domain: a hospital outpatient department (OPD). A doctor has a department (General Medicine, Cardiology, Dermatology, Pediatrics, Orthopedics, ENT), a phone, an email, a consultation fee in rupees, a room and an availability (Available, On leave). A doctor has appointments (patient, phone, date, time, reason, status Booked / Completed / Cancelled; a finished or cancelled appointment cannot change again), prescriptions (patient, medicine, dosage, days 1 to 90, instructions) and lab tests (patient, test Blood test / X-ray / MRI / ECG / Urine test, cost in rupees, status Ordered / Collected / Reported, always in that order). Phone numbers are 10-digit Indian mobiles; a doctor's email is unique. Dashboard: appointments per doctor and by status, doctors by department, lab revenue, average consultation fee.
