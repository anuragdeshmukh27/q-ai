Domain: events and fests. An event has a name, category, venue, date, start time, capacity and an entry fee in rupees. People register for it (student, college, email, phone, status Registered / Checked in / Cancelled), volunteers staff it (role, shift) and sponsors fund it (company, contact, amount in rupees, status Pledged / Paid). Registrations stop when the event is full (409). Emails are unique within an event. Phone numbers are 10-digit Indian mobiles. Dashboard: registrations per event, sponsorship received, checked-in count.
