Domain: cabs and rides. Drivers have a phone, a car model, a plate number (unique), a car type (Mini / Sedan / SUV) and a rating from 1 to 5. Each driver has rides (passenger, phone, pickup, drop, pickup time, fare in rupees greater than 0, status Requested / Accepted / On trip / Completed / Cancelled; a completed or cancelled ride cannot change again). Actions on a ride set its status (Complete, Cancel). Dashboard: fare earned from completed rides, rides by status, rides per driver. Not built: live GPS, matching, surge pricing, payments.
