Database Engineer / Data Engineer
Meridian Health Analytics, healthcare data company, Hybrid

About the role
Meridian Health Analytics turns hospital and clinic records into reports that care teams rely on every morning. As a database engineer you will look after the schemas, the data pipelines and the quality of the patient data behind those reports.

What you will do
- Design and evolve relational schemas in PostgreSQL: data modeling, constraints and indexing for speed and for integrity.
- Write and review database migrations, including migrations that have to fix bad production data without losing a single row.
- Build and run ETL data pipelines (Python, SQL, Airflow, dbt) into our data warehouse.
- Troubleshoot data incidents: duplicated records, broken constraints, slow queries. Find the root cause before you change anything.
- Add tests for your queries and migrations, and keep the data tests green in CI.
- Explain data problems in plain words to analysts and to the clinical operations team, and tell them what you changed.

What we are looking for
- 4+ years of experience with SQL and relational databases; query optimization experience.
- Python for data work.
- A careful, data-first way of working: you look at the data before you write the fix.
- Ownership of the databases you build, including being reachable when they misbehave.
